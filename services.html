How we work

The same four steps on every deal, so you always know what comes next.

  1. Tell us what you need

    A short call or WhatsApp chat about your budget, location and timeline.

  2. See verified options

    We send only properties whose titles and prices we have checked.

  3. Inspect and decide

    Visit at your pace. We share the full costs in writing before you commit.

  4. Close safely

    We coordinate lawyers and transfer until the keys or title are in your hands.

Common questions

How do you verify a title?

We run an official search, confirm the seller's identity and check for caveats or disputes. You get a copy of the result.

What do you charge?

Fees are agreed in writing before we start. There are no hidden charges, and we explain every cost in the deal.

Can I view properties on weekends?

Yes. We schedule viewings Monday to Saturday and can arrange Sunday visits on request.

Do you help with a mortgage?

We introduce you to lenders and explain your options. The choice of lender is always yours.
